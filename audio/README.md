# Boss Music Audio Directory

Place your audio tracks here (e.g. `phase1.mp3`, `phase2.mp3`, `boss_theme.wav`).

In `asset-test.html` (Tab 4: Boss Music Editor) or in your presets, reference them by their URL path relative to the public root:
- `/audio/phase1.mp3`
- `/audio/phase2.mp3`
- `/audio/boss_theme.mp3`
